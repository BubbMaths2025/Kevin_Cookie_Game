# Kevin's Cookie Shop Tycoon

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No installation, internet connection, or server is required.

You start with $100 and have 30 days to grow your cookie shop. Each day, choose a batch size, price, and advertising budget, then open the shop to see your results.

- Each cookie costs $0.50 to bake. Daily shop costs are $5.
- All costs must be affordable before opening.
- Lower prices attract more customers; advertising increases demand with diminishing returns.
- The day's forecast affects demand. There is also a little daily randomness.
- Unsold cookies expire at the end of the day.
- The season ends after day 30, or when cash falls below the $5 needed to open.
- Progress is saved in browser storage when available. Use Start over to reset.

Files: `index.html` (page), `style.css` (design), `game.js` (simulation and save state).

The animated shop comes to life when you open each day. Roughly one visitor appears for every five cookies sold (up to 60 visitors), with different skin tones, hairstyles, clothes, and ages. Your apron-wearing shopkeeper serves them cookies. You can skip or replay the scene; reduced-motion preferences show the sales summary immediately. Sales are saved before the animation, so closing the tab never loses the day.
